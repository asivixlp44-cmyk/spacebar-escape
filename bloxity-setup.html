Short Description

Pass a gate for Wins, unlock faster treadmills, hatch space pets from Star Capsules, rebirth for a multiplier and race in Black Hole Rush.
Description
Every step you take is +1 Speed and +1 XP. XP fills your Level, and your Level is how fast you can walk (the WALK box lets you walk slower any time, up to your max). You land on the Starport, a station deck floating in space: train on its treadmills (hold DASH to sprint; the Wins you earn open faster ones, from the Moon Treadmill up to the Pulsar: x20 every step), then run through the launch gantry onto the Keyway: a giant mechanical keyboard floating north through the stars, its keys clacking down under your feet, cut into fifteen stages by warp gates marked STAGE 1 to STAGE 15. The gates are always open, and a Recommended Level holo tells you when you're ready for the next one. Every stage is a challenge of its own: spinning laser beams to jump, sliding force gates to slip past, plasma rifts with key bridges, one narrow skybridge, giant floating keys to hop (too slow and you fall short), glitch keys that blink out behind you and jump pads that launch you across. Touch a laser or the plasma and you're back at the Starport in a shield bubble, with the offer to revive straight back into the stage you reached: free every 3 minutes, otherwise 5 Gems. Every gate you pass pays Wins, triple the first time each rebirth. Spend them on upgrades, trails and Star Capsules for forty space pets that follow you around (moon bunnies, rocket pups, little aliens, a Black Hole Dragon), and get auras with Gems. Rebirth at Level 10 (then 15, 20 and on) for a permanent Speed and XP multiplier. Clear all fifteen stages to open the next sector: Home Row Orbit, Redshift Ridge, Frostbyte Rings and Esc Horizon, each under its own sky: the blue planet you orbit, a red giant, a ringed ice giant, a black hole. Press RACE for Black Hole Rush: six lanes, the black hole rolling in behind, first out wins. Quests, daily tasks, a daily reward, free playtime gifts, a lucky wheel, codes and leaderboards. How to play: run with WASD, the arrow keys, the on-screen stick or a controller; Space or JUMP to jump; Shift or DASH for a burst of speed. Plays held upright or sideways on a phone. No in-app purchases and no Bux needed: everything is earned in the game.

Hosting steps

  1. GitHub: create the empty repo LEGiON-Platforms/spacebar-escape (no README) and add the repo secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN.
  2. hosting.bloxity.io: the app spacebar-escape must exist (its id is GAME_ID in .github/workflows/deploy.yml).
  3. Push the code to main (as asivixlp44-cmyk): the workflow builds the server image and the client and deploys both.
  4. Check https://spacebar-escape.host.bloxity.io/health (it should say "db": true: Legion provides MONGODB_URI) and play at https://spacebar-escape.play.bloxity.io.
  5. Create the game in Manage Games with the values above (slug exactly spacebar-escape).
